Editorial Policy & Mission

About Savings Tactic

SavingsTactic.com is an independent consumer resource built to replace vague "get rich quick" advice and confusing coupon hype with transparent math, browser-based calculators, and honest reviews of money-saving and micro-earning tools.

Our Four Core Standards

1. Out-of-Pocket Cash Comes First

We never advertise an item as "free" or "$0.05" if you have to pay $15 out of pocket at the register and wait weeks for an app rebate to clear. Every calculator and guide separates immediate checkout cost from pending rebates.

2. Primary-Source Policy Verification

Retailer digital coupon terms, BOGO rules, and cashback payout thresholds are checked directly against published corporate terms and updated with visible "Last Updated" timestamps.

3. Realistic Earning Expectations

Cashback portals, receipt-scanning apps, and survey sites are useful for offsetting household bills—not replacing a full-time paycheck. We state realistic payout minimums, cashout methods (PayPal, ACH, gift cards), and time tradeoffs upfront.

4. Clear Monetization & Affiliate Disclosure

All web calculators on Savings Tactic are 100% free to use without creating an account. We fund site development through optional digital spreadsheets on Gumroad, Amazon Associates links, and clearly labeled referral links to cashback platforms we personally evaluate.

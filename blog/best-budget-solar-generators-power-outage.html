How to Stretch a 1,024Wh Battery for 3+ Days Without Solar

1. Duty-Cycle Your Fridge

Don't leave your fridge plugged in 24/7 during a multi-day outage. Run it for 2 hours until chilled to 35°F, then unplug it for 4 hours (keep the door closed). This cuts daily watt-hour usage by more than half.

2. Turn Off CPAP Humidifier Heat

A CPAP machine with its heated humidifier and heated hose draws 60W–90W (draining a battery overnight). Turning off heat drops draw to 12W–18W, giving you 4 to 5 full nights on a 1,024Wh unit.

3. Use DC/USB-C Ports Over AC

Turning on the 120V AC inverter consumes 15W–25W of idle power even with nothing plugged in. Charge phones, laptops, and rechargeable lanterns directly from the USB-C / 12V DC ports and keep AC switched off when idle.

CVS 98% Rule & ExtraBucks Spend-Threshold Calculator

The Short Answer: Under the CVS 98% rule, your qualifying subtotal only needs to hit 98% of the required spend amount (for example, $29.40 on a Spend $30 deal). However, CVS store coupons (CRTs) subtract from your threshold, whereas manufacturer coupons and ExtraBucks do not. Test your exact basket below before scanning at the register.

2026 Store POS Update Warning:

While the majority of CVS registers still honor the 98% buffer, some stores running updated point-of-sale software enforce a strict 100% spend threshold. In addition, CVS online pickup/shipping orders often calculate thresholds differently than in-store registers. Always check your receipt immediately at checkout.

CVS Clearance Markdown Schedule 2026: Seasonal Drops, Planogram Resets & Scanner Hacks

By Savings Tactic Editorial Team • Updated November 13, 2026 • Verified In-Store Clearance Mechanics

If you only shop CVS weekly circulars, you are missing out on the chain's deepest discounts: 75% to 90% off seasonal markdowns and unadvertised planogram reset clearance. Unlike Target, CVS does not follow a rigid chainwide day-of-the-week markdown calendar—local store managers activate price changes on varying weekdays.

Instead, winning at CVS clearance comes down to tracking two predictable cycles: post-holiday seasonal step-downs and aisle planogram resets.

1. How the 50% → 75% → 90% Seasonal Clearance Cycle Works

For major holidays (Christmas, Valentine's Day, Easter, Summer/Patio, Back-to-School, and Halloween), CVS steps remaining seasonal merchandise down in predictable waves:

Timeline After Holiday Non-Food / Decor / Gifts Seasonal Candy & Snacks
Day 1 After Holiday 50% Off 30% – 50% Off
Days 7 – 10 75% Off 50% – 75% Off
Days 14 – 21 (Final Drop) 90% Off 75% – 90% Off (store dependent)

Insider Tip: Check everyday household aisles for seasonal packaging (such as holiday-themed Ziploc bags, Kleenex tissues, paper plates, and Glad trash bags)—they drop to 75%–90% off right alongside holiday decor!

2. Spotting Unadvertised Planogram Reset Clearance (Year-Round)

Every month, CVS resets specific aisles (cosmetics, hair care, skincare, vitamins, or oral care) to make room for new product launches. Discontinued SKUs step down from 25% → 50% → 75% off directly on the regular aisle shelves before being pulled.

  • Use the Red ExtraCare Coupon Center Scanner: Store staff frequently fall behind on printing yellow clearance tags. Whenever you see an entire shelf row looking sparse or items pushed to a back endcap, scan the barcode at the in-store ExtraCare Coupon Center kiosk—it displays the exact live register price.
  • Stack ExtraBucks & Manufacturer Coupons on Clearance: Unlike percentage-off CRTs (which exclude clearance items), you can pay for 75%–90% clearance hauls using your ExtraBucks rewards and eligible manufacturer digital coupons.
  • Don't Expect ExtraBucks to Print on Clearance: Corporate policy generally excludes clearance-priced items from triggering new ExtraBucks promotions, so use clearance hauls as the final burn transaction for expiring ExtraBucks.

CVS Clearance FAQ

Can I return clearance items bought at CVS?

Most seasonal and final-clearance markdowns (75%–90% off) are final sale or subject to store manager discretion. Always verify prices at the ExtraCare price checker before buying.

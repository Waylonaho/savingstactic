1. Weekly Department Markdown Schedule

CVS stores operate on an automated price adjustment rotation. While individual franchise managers may make minor adjustments, central POS terminals trigger markdown batches on specific weekdays:

Day of Week Departments Marked Down Typical Discount
Sunday Weekly Ad Turnover, Seasonal Items, Toys 30% – 50% Off
Tuesday Cosmetics, Skincare, Beauty & Hair Products 50% – 75% Off
Thursday Household Supplies, Paper Products, Groceries 50% – 75% Off
Friday Final Clearance Drops, Discontinued Overstock 75% – 90% Off

2. How the 50% ➔ 75% ➔ 90% Seasonal Drop Works

For major holidays (Halloween, Christmas, Valentine's Day, Easter, Summer/Back to School), CVS follows a strict tiered clearance lifecycle:

  • Day After Holiday: Non-candy items and decor immediately drop to 50% off (candy typically sits at 33%–50% off).
  • Day 7–10: Inventory automatically drops to 75% off to clear shelf space.
  • Day 14+: Any remaining stock is marked down to 90% off. Whatever does not sell is removed from inventory.

3. Secret Pro Hack: The Price Tag Scanner Trick

Store employees often get behind on manually placing yellow discount stickers on every single product. If you see seasonal items sitting on back endcaps, always take them to the red in-store ExtraCare Coupon Machine or use the in-app price scanner. Nine times out of ten, the product will ring up at 75% or 90% off in the central computer even if the shelf tag says full price!

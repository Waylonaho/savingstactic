5 Steps to Fix Kiosk Printing Failures

  1. Switch to Digital Clips: Open the CVS Mobile App and tap "Send to Card" on all target rewards.
  2. Re-enter Phone Number: Type your phone number twice slowly at the kiosk scanner window.
  3. Check Account Merges: Ensure your phone number isn't tied to a duplicate old ExtraCare card.
  4. Ask Manager for Manual Issue: Cashiers can manually issue missing ExtraBucks at the register terminal.
  5. Call ExtraCare Customer Support: Dial 1-800-SHOP-CVS to have missing rewards credited to your app balance.

CVS Coupon Center Not Printing Coupons? 5 Quick Fixes

By Savings Tactic Editorial Team • Published October 13, 2026

Scanning your card at the red CVS Coupon Center kiosk only to see the dreaded "No coupons available for this card today" message is a common frustration for drugstore couponers. If your CVS coupon center is not printing coupons, the issue is rarely a broken machine; it is almost always caused by account routing, Sunday server reload windows, or paperless digital settings.

Because CVS has transitioned the majority of its customized register tape offers (CRTs) into the ExtraCare mobile app, understanding how paper and digital delivery interact is critical before you plan your weekly shopping trip.

1. Digital-Only Account Setting Is Enabled

If you opted into "Digital Receipts" inside the CVS app or selected digital-only coupons at checkout, the physical RedBox terminal will automatically suppress paper printing.

The Fix: Open the CVS mobile app, navigate to Account Settings > Receipt Preferences, and verify whether paper receipts are toggled off. Even if the RedBox prints nothing, all of your personalized CRT store coupons are waiting inside the "Deals & Rewards" tab of your app—ready to be sent to your card with a single tap.

2. The "Scan Twice" Rule

Experienced couponers know that a single scan rarely pulls everything. Because CVS servers queue print batches in groups of 3 to 5 vouchers, the screen may tell you that no more coupons exist after printing the first set.

  • Scan your ExtraCare card barcode.
  • Collect your printed slips.
  • Scan the card a second (and third) time until the screen explicitly displays "All coupons printed."

3. Sunday Morning Server Refresh Windows

New CVS weekly sales cycle every Sunday. Corporate servers push personalized store coupon algorithms between 12:00 AM and 6:00 AM local time. If you scan your card early on Sunday morning, your account may not have populated with new weekly CRTs yet.

If clipped offers fail to deduct later at the register, follow our receipt diagnostic guide in CVS Digital Coupon Not Working? A Receipt-First Troubleshooting Guide.

4. Card Number Mismatch (Phone Number vs Physical Barcode)

Typing your phone number at the kiosk can access a duplicate, secondary account created years ago under a landline or old email address.

Always scan the barcode directly from the CVS mobile app screen rather than entering your phone number. This guarantees you are accessing the specific account holding your clipped digital coupons and earned ExtraBucks.

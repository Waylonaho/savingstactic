1. How to Read Your Receipt's Coupon Section

At the bottom of every CVS receipt, just above the subtotal, you will find a dedicated section titled "COUPONS APPLIED".

  • MFG COUPONS: Shows all clipped digital and paper manufacturer coupons. If your manufacturer discount is missing here, the register never recognized the product barcode as an eligible match.
  • CVS COUPONS: Displays store coupons (CRTs), app bonuses, and percentage-off discounts.
  • EXTRACARE REWARDS: Details any ExtraBucks applied as cash tender. If your rewards expired, see our recovery checklist on What to Do When CVS ExtraBucks Expire.

2. The Top 4 Causes of Missing Digital Discounts

Problem Receipt Symptom Immediate Fix
Account Desync Card number on receipt doesn't match App Card ID Check for duplicate family accounts linked to your phone number.
Size Exclusions Item rings up at full shelf price with no MFG line Scan item barcode in CVS app prior to checkout to verify eligibility.
Threshold Deficit Store sale price dropped total below coupon threshold Add a small filler item to hit the pre-tax threshold requirement.
Clipping Lag Coupon appears clipped in app but absent on receipt Clip coupons at least 15 minutes before approaching the register.

3. How to Request an Immediate Post-Checkout Adjustment

If you notice a clipped digital coupon failed to attach while standing at the register, do not leave the store:

  1. Show the cashier the clipped coupon on your active CVS app screen showing the eligible barcode.
  2. Cashiers can issue a manual manufacturer coupon credit or apply a supervisor courtesy adjustment right at the register.
  3. If an item must be refunded, review our guide on How Returns and ExtraBucks Refunds Work so you don't lose prorated rewards in the process.

CVS ExtraCare vs. myWalgreens Rewards: Which Drugstore Program Wins in 2026?

By Savings Tactic Editorial Team • Updated November 13, 2026 • Verified 2026 Loyalty Terms

Both CVS ExtraCare and myWalgreens are free to join and rely on stacking digital manufacturer coupons with weekly store promotions. However, their register math works completely differently once you start redeeming rewards.

The Bottom-Line Verdict: CVS ExtraCare wins for weekly coupon stackers because ExtraBucks roll seamlessly into new Spend deals and obey the 98% threshold rule. myWalgreens wins for casual, non-coupon shoppers who buy store-brand health goods (5% back) and want a full 12 months before rewards expire.

1. Why CVS ExtraCare Wins for Active Deal Stackers

At CVS, ExtraBucks function like pure cash tender at the register. You can buy Toothpaste Deal A in Transaction #1, earn $5 ExtraBucks, and immediately hand over that $5 ExtraBucks to pay for Body Wash Deal B in Transaction #2—and still print $4 in brand-new ExtraBucks. Learn the step-by-step sequence in our CVS ExtraBucks Hacks 2026 Guide.

Additionally, CVS enforces the 98% rule on Spend thresholds (so $29.40 satisfies a $30 requirement), whereas Walgreens requires hitting 100.0% of the threshold down to the penny.

2. Why myWalgreens Wins for Casual & Store-Brand Shoppers

If you hate tracking 30-day coupon expiration clocks, myWalgreens is far lower maintenance. Walgreens Cash sits safely in your digital wallet for up to 12 months. And because Walgreens pays 5% back on Walgreens-branded products (compared to CVS's free 2% base rate), spending just 25% of your annual drugstore budget on store-brand vitamins, allergy relief, or paper goods puts myWalgreens ahead of free CVS ExtraCare.

Just watch out for Walgreens' register trap: never redeem Walgreens Cash on a "Spend $20, Get $5 Walgreens Cash" promotion unless your remaining cash balance after coupons still equals $20.00, or the booster will fail to trigger. Instead, redeem Walgreens Cash on "Buy 2, Get $4 Register Rewards" item-count deals!

CVS vs. Walgreens Rewards FAQ

Do both CVS and Walgreens offer prescription rewards?

Yes, in eligible states (excluding AR, NJ, and NY). CVS pays $2 in ExtraBucks for every 4 eligible prescription credits (up to $50/year after opting in), while Walgreens pays 1% Walgreens Cash on eligible out-of-pocket pharmacy spend.

Is CVS ExtraCare Plus ($5/month) worth paying for?

If you shop at CVS at least once a month, paying $5/month unlocks an instant $10 monthly ExtraBucks promo reward (a $5 net monthly profit), free same-day/standard shipping on eligible orders, and 20% off CVS Health brand products.

Do CVS Coupons Count Toward ExtraBucks Spend Deals?

By Savings Tactic Editorial Team • Published October 8, 2026

One of the most frequent questions from deal hunters is: do CVS coupons count toward ExtraBucks spend deals? If a weekly circular promotion advertises "Spend $30 on select beauty, get $10 ExtraBucks," will using $15 worth of manufacturer coupons prevent your $10 reward from printing at checkout?

The short answer is yes, manufacturer coupons do count toward the spend threshold because CVS calculates promotional thresholds based on your pre-coupon retail total. However, how store coupons (CRTs) and ExtraBucks rewards interact with threshold requirements requires careful math.

1. Spend Deals vs. Buy Deals: Register Calculations

Understanding how different promotion styles calculate totals determines how you layer discounts:

  • Buy Deals (e.g., "Buy 2, Get $5 ExtraBucks"): Quantity-driven. As long as the two qualifying items are scanned, coupons of any kind do not interfere with the reward printing.
  • Spend Deals (e.g., "Spend $30, Get $10 ExtraBucks"): Dollar-driven. The register monitors your retail subtotal. Manufacturer coupons (digital or paper) reduce your out-of-pocket cash expense, but the register still credits the full product price toward the $30 threshold.

2. The Famous CVS "98% Rule"

CVS stores operate on an automated 98% threshold rule programmed into central register software. If a deal requires spending $30, the POS computer triggers the reward once your qualifying subtotal reaches $29.40 (98% of $30.00).

Worked Example: If your qualifying beauty items total $29.60, the register registers the deal as fulfilled. You can apply $12 in manufacturer coupons and pay with ExtraBucks; your $10 reward slip will still print at the bottom of the receipt.

3. Where CVS Rules Differ from Walgreens

CVS is vastly more forgiving with coupon stacking than competitor drugstores. While Walgreens cancels bonus rewards if you redeem Walgreens Cash on threshold deals (explained in our Walgreens Cash Spend Deals Guide), CVS treats ExtraBucks as liquid tender. You can pay your remaining balance with ExtraBucks earned last week without voiding your new spend reward.

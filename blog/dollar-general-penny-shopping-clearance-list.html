1. How Dollar General Penny Shopping Works

Dollar General operates on a strict corporate inventory markdown schedule. When seasonal merchandise (such as summer patio gear, holiday decor, or discontinued grocery and health items) fails to sell at 50% or 90% off clearance, the system automatically reduces the price to $0.01.

Historically, penny items were meant to be pulled from shelves by store associates and returned or discarded. However, if items are left sitting on endcaps or bottom shelves, they will scan for one penny at the cash register.

2. The Tuesday Morning Markdown Schedule

System-wide price updates and penny markdowns process overnight and take effect on Tuesday mornings before store doors open. Experienced clearance shoppers scout stores early on Tuesdays to catch fresh markdowns before other local hunters or employees clear the aisles.

Important Register Etiquette:

Never argue with cashiers if a penny item fails to scan or if a manager refuses to sell an unpulled item. Dollar General corporate policy encourages managers to pull recalled or discontinued stock; forcing a sale on recalled goods or arguing can result in store bans. Always be polite, respectful, and move on to another location if needed.

Dollar General Penny Shopping List 2026: Clearance Dot Schedule & How to Find $0.01 Items

By Savings Tactic Editorial Team • Updated November 12, 2026 • Verified DG Markdown Rules

Dollar General penny shopping is the practice of finding discontinued seasonal, home, apparel, or health products that have cycled down to $0.01 (one cent) in the corporate point-of-sale system. When Dollar General phases out seasonal merchandise, prices step down from 50% off to 70% off, 90% off, and finally to $0.01 so store staff know to pull the remaining inventory from shelves.

If store associates miss pulling an item before you bring it to the register, Dollar General corporate policy requires cashiers to sell you the penny items in your cart for $0.01 each.

1. How to Decode Dollar General Colored Dots & Price Tag Symbols

Dollar General prints small colored dots, shapes, and seasonal codes directly on product price tags and cardboard packaging. Watching which tag category just hit 70% or 90% off tells you exactly which items are next in line to drop to $0.01:

Tag / Dot Indicator Product Category Where to Hunt in Store
Blue / Grey / Brown Dots Apparel, Socks, Slippers & Seasonal Clothing Hanging clothing racks, endcap bins, and top-shelf overstock totes
Green / Yellow / Pink Dots Home Decor, Kitchenwares, Towels & Storage Home aisle top shelves ("sky shelves") and seasonal transition aisles
Seasonal Icons (Snowflake, Sun, Leaf) Holiday, Summer BBQ, Back-to-School & Lawn/Garden Center seasonal aisle, clearance endcaps, and outdoor patio displays
Orange Clearance Stickers Discontinued Core Grocery, Health & Beauty (POG Reset) Regular inline aisles (look for white/orange tags mixed behind new stock)

2. How to Verify Penny Items Using the Dollar General App

Never rely on paper shelf tags—employees rarely put "$0.01" stickers on penny merchandise. Follow this 4-step scanning workflow inside the store:

  1. Set Your Exact Store Location First: Open the DG app, tap the store address bar at the top, and select the exact store you are standing in. Because Dollar General uses regional price tiers, scanning under the wrong store pin can show inaccurate prices. (See our guide on Dollar General App Price Higher at Checkout if prices mismatch.)
  2. Open the Barcode Scanner: Tap the barcode scan icon inside the DG app search bar.
  3. Scan the Product UPC (Not the Shelf Strip): Aim your camera at the barcode printed on the item itself.
  4. Read the Price Readout: If the screen displays $0.01 or $0.00, the item is a verified penny item at that location. Note that app inventory counts ("Out of Stock") are frequently delayed—trust the $0.01 price readout when the item is in your hand.

3. Unwritten Rules & Corporate Policy of Dollar General Penny Hunting

  • Never Ask Employees About Penny Items: Penny items are supposed to be pulled from the floor by staff before customers find them. If you ask an employee where the penny items are, they are required to pull the stock off the shelf immediately. Use your DG app scanner quietly.
  • Shop Early on Tuesday Mornings: Corporate price files push to store registers overnight Monday into Tuesday. Arriving at 8:00 AM Tuesday gives you the best window before staff finish pulling discontinued SKUs.
  • Use Self-Checkout or Stay Polite at the Register: Self-checkout kiosks ring up $0.01 items automatically without friction. If you check out with a cashier and a manager resists selling the item, stay polite and cite Dollar General's customer-first pricing policy—cashiers must sell what is already in your cart, though they are allowed to pull remaining items off the sales floor afterward.
  • Leave No Mess Behind: Never tear open boxes or leave unorganized piles on clearance endcaps. Keeping aisles tidy ensures local store managers remain friendly toward couponers.

Dollar General Penny Shopping FAQ

Can I use digital coupons on $0.01 penny items?

No. Digital manufacturer coupons require the item price to be equal to or greater than the coupon value, and Dollar General registers do not give overage cash back. However, penny items still count toward your item count if you are filling out a cart.

Do all Dollar General stores have the exact same penny items?

Not always. While seasonal dot markdowns (like holiday or summer goods) usually drop nationwide on the same Tuesday, core grocery and health reset items can vary by regional distribution center and store pricing tier.

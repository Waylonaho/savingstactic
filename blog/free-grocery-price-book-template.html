What Is a Grocery Price-Book?

A grocery price-book is a tracking log of the lowest regular and promotional unit prices for items your household buys regularly. Instead of relying on store sale tags—which often disguise price hikes with "2 for $6" promotions—a price-book breaks down costs to common units: price per ounce ($/oz), price per pound ($/lb), price per load ($/load), or price per square foot.

1. How to Calculate Final Unit Price After an Instant Coupon vs. Pending Cashback

When comparing two grocery items, combining an immediate register coupon and a post-checkout app rebate into a single discount number can distort your real grocery budget. Instant digital or paper coupons lower the actual amount you pay at the register today, whereas rebate apps (like Ibotta or Fetch) hold funds until you reach a minimum withdrawal balance (such as Ibotta's $20.00 minimum payout threshold, checked October 2026).

1. Shelf Unit Price = Shelf Price ÷ Base Quantity
2. Checkout Unit Price = (Shelf Price − Confirmed Instant Coupon) ÷ Base Quantity
3. Post-Rebate Estimate = (Checkout Price − Approved Rebate) ÷ Base Quantity

How to Calculate Unit Price (Formula & Step-by-Step)

A product's unit price is the cost per standard unit of weight, volume, or count. By converting products of different package dimensions into a single shared measurement, shoppers can eliminate marketing illusions and identify the true cost.

Unit Price = (Total Shelf Price − Clipped Coupons) ÷ Package Quantity

Example: A 24-ounce box of cereal costs $4.99, while a 14-ounce box costs $2.99 with a $0.50 digital coupon:

  • 24 oz Box: $4.99 ÷ 24 oz = $0.208 per ounce
  • 14 oz Box: ($2.99 − $0.50) ÷ 14 oz = $0.178 per ounce

Even though the 24 oz box looks like a better family deal on the shelf, the coupon on the smaller 14 oz box makes it 14.4% cheaper per ounce.

1. How to Calculate Final Unit Price After an Instant Coupon vs. Pending Cashback

When comparing two grocery items, combining an immediate register coupon and a post-checkout app rebate into a single discount number can distort your real grocery budget. Instant digital or paper coupons lower the actual amount you pay at the register today, whereas rebate apps (like Ibotta or Fetch) hold funds until you reach a minimum withdrawal balance (such as Ibotta's $20.00 minimum payout threshold, checked October 2026).

1. Shelf Unit Price = Shelf Price ÷ Base Quantity
2. Checkout Unit Price = (Shelf Price − Confirmed Instant Coupon) ÷ Base Quantity
3. Post-Rebate Estimate = (Checkout Price − Approved Rebate) ÷ Base Quantity

2. How to Compare Unit Prices When Package Sizes Differ (4 Worked Examples)

Retailers frequently label one shelf tag per ounce and the neighboring tag per pound or per count. Use these four standard conversions to normalize any aisle comparison:

1. Weight Conversion (1 lb = 16 oz)

Shredded Cheese: 8 oz vs. 2 lb Block

Multiply pounds by 16 to compare everything in ounces:

  • 8 oz bag @ $2.29 = $2.29 ÷ 8 = $0.286 / oz
  • 2 lb (32 oz) block @ $7.36 = $7.36 ÷ 32 = $0.230 / oz
2. Liquid Volume (1 Gal = 128 fl oz)

Orange Juice: 64 fl oz vs. 1 Gallon

Convert gallons (128 fl oz) and quarts (32 fl oz) into fluid ounces:

  • 64 fl oz ($3.99 − $1.00 cpn) = $2.99 ÷ 64 = $0.047 / fl oz
  • 1 Gallon (128 fl oz) @ $6.49 = $6.49 ÷ 128 = $0.051 / fl oz
3. Count / Laundry Loads (Per Pod/Load)

Detergent Pacs: 42 ct vs. 84 ct Tub

Compare detergent by actual wash loads or pac count rather than bottle weight:

  • 42 ct ($14.99 − $3.00 cpn) = $11.99 ÷ 42 = $0.285 / ct
  • 84 ct tub @ $21.99 (no cpn) = $21.99 ÷ 84 = $0.262 / ct
4. Paper Goods (Per 100 Sheets / Sq Ft)

Paper Towels: "Double" vs. "Mega" Rolls

Ignore "roll math" claims and divide by total sheets (per 100 sheets):

  • 6 rolls × 120 sheets (720 sheets) @ $7.99 net = $1.110 / 100 sheets
  • 12 rolls × 85 sheets (1,020 sheets) @ $13.99 = $1.372 / 100 sheets

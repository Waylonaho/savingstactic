How to Calculate True Grocery Unit Price

Store shelf tags frequently display mismatched units—listing one pasta brand per ounce and the bag right next to it per pound. Converting both items to the same base measurement reveals the true cost per unit before you reach the register.

1. Shelf Unit Price = Shelf Price ÷ Normalized Base Units
2. Checkout Unit Price = (Shelf Price − Instant Coupon) ÷ Base Units
3. Post-Rebate Estimate = (Checkout Price − Pending App Rebate) ÷ Base Units

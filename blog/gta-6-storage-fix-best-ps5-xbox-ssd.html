GTA 6 Storage & Pre-Load FAQ

Does installing an NVMe SSD void my PS5 warranty?

No. Sony officially designed the PS5 side panel and M.2 expansion bay for user installation and provides step-by-step instructions on PlayStation Support.

Should I buy a 1TB or 2TB SSD for GTA 6?

Go with 2TB if your budget allows. Because GTA 6 alone takes ~212–225 GB (plus future GTA Online updates), a 1TB drive loses nearly a quarter of its capacity immediately, whereas 2TB routinely costs only $35–$50 more per terabyte.

1. The Points-to-Pump Conversion Scale

Fuel points redeem in increments of 100 points. At Kroger Fuel Centers, discounts apply up to 35 gallons per fill-up:

Fuel Points Redeemed Discount Per Gallon Max Savings (20 Gallon Fill-up)
100 Points $0.10 / gallon $2.00
500 Points $0.50 / gallon $10.00
1,000 Points $1.00 / gallon $20.00

2. The 4x Gift Card Accelerator Strategy

Standard grocery spending earns 1 point per $1 spent. However, Kroger routinely runs digital coupon promotions offering 4x Fuel Points on gift card purchases:

  • Purchasing a $250 third-party gift card (such as Home Depot, Amazon, or dining) during a 4x event earns 1,000 Fuel Points instantly.
  • That $250 purchase immediately produces a $1.00/gallon discount on your next fuel purchase.
  • By pairing this with store promotions on everyday household groceries, your fuel discounts subsidize your weekly grocery spend. Review our Grocery Budgeting Strategy for additional tips.

3. Expiration Cycles: How to Avoid Leaving Points on the Table

Kroger Fuel Points expire on the last day of the month following the month they were earned. For example, any points earned during October expire November 30th. Points earned in separate calendar months do not combine at the pump; the register automatically pulls from the month with the highest point balance.

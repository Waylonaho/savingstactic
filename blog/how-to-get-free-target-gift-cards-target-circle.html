1. Triggering "Gift Card With Purchase" Promos

Target runs rotating weekly department promotions where purchasing a specific dollar amount or quantity triggers a promotional digital gift card. These cards are added automatically to your Target app Wallet at checkout:

  • Household Essentials: Spend $50 on laundry, paper towels, and trash bags, get a $15 gift card.
  • Beauty & Personal Care: Buy 3 select personal care items, get a $5 gift card.
  • Baby Department: Spend $100 on diapers and wipes, get a $20–$30 gift card.

2. The Triple-Stack Savings Rule

Target's POS registers allow you to combine three distinct discount tiers on the same transaction:

  1. Target Circle Manufacturer Coupons: Clipped digitally inside the Target app.
  2. Target Category Promo: The department-level gift card offer.
  3. Target Circle Card (Formerly RedCard) / Reward Gift Card: Pay with a promotional gift card or earn 5% off the remaining balance.

3. Speeding Up Redemptions via Consumer Study Panels

Instead of waiting weeks for in-app spending thresholds to accrue points, high-intent shoppers participate in single opt-in market research panels. These research initiatives compensate participants with direct entries toward high-value grocery and retail shopping cards, which can be applied directly alongside your Target Circle Wallet barcode.

Can I gameshare GTA 6 on PS5 or Xbox Series X|S to split the $79.99 cost?

Yes. Using PlayStation's official Console Sharing and Offline Play or Xbox's Home Xbox feature lets two family consoles share a single digital copy—cutting your effective launch cost down to $39.99 per person.

Rockstar includes the Vintage Vice City Pack (the yellow '55 Vapid Stanier sedan, private garage, and exclusive outfits for Jason and Lucia) on purchases made before November 20, 2026.

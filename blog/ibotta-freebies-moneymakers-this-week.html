2. How to Avoid the "Dual-Claim" Rejection Rule

If you shop at Walmart, remember that Walmart Cash and Ibotta use the exact same backend rebate engine. You cannot claim Walmart Cash on a product in the Walmart app and then submit the paper receipt to Ibotta for the same barcode. Doing so will result in an automated rebate clawback.

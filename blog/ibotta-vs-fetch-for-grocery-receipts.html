Ibotta vs. Fetch for Grocery Receipts: Which Pays More in 2026?

By Savings Tactic Editorial Team • Published October 2026

When choosing the best receipt app for groceries, the debate almost always comes down to Ibotta vs Fetch. While both allow you to turn paper and digital receipts into retail gift cards, their reward structures reward completely different shopping behaviors.

Ibotta requires you to manually select high-value product offers before scanning, delivering $0.50 to $5.00+ on specific brand items. Fetch takes an automated approach: snap any receipt from any store, earn baseline points instantly, and collect automatic bonuses whenever partner brands appear on your ticket.

1. How Ibotta Payouts Work

Ibotta is built for targeted grocery savings. If you regularly buy major household and packaged food brands (Kraft, General Mills, Colgate, etc.), individual offers range from $0.75 to $3.00 per item. However, because you need $20 to withdraw, casual shoppers buying store generics can take months to cash out. Also, keep in mind how third-party networks intersect—if you shop at Walmart, read our guide on Walmart Cash and Ibotta Stacking Rules to avoid dual-claim rejections.

2. How Fetch Rewards Points Work

Fetch Rewards operates purely on points (1,000 points is roughly equal to $1.00 in gift card value). Every receipt from any grocery store, gas station, or convenience store awards at least 25 points. Special brand partner promos yield 1,000 to 3,000 bonus points. Because you can redeem digital gift cards starting at roughly 3,000 to 5,000 points ($3–$5), Fetch lets you redeem rewards much sooner than Ibotta.

3. The Double-Stack Secret: Scan Both Apps with the Same Receipt

You do not have to choose between Ibotta and Fetch. Because they run on independent verification networks, you can scan the exact same grocery receipt into both apps:

  • First, check Ibotta for specific brand rebates before you shop.
  • After checkout, upload your receipt to Ibotta to claim your large cash-back items.
  • Immediately scan that same receipt into Fetch to grab automated brand points and base scan credit.

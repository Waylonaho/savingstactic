2. Who Should Skip Target Circle 360?

If your goal is cutting monthly grocery and household expenses, free Target Circle gives you all of the coupon and deal features. You still receive automatic app deals at checkout, access to personalized Target Circle Bonus missions, and free curbside Drive Up without any order minimums[span_16](start_span)[span_16](end_span).

Paying $99 a year is essentially an upfront subscription to Shipt home delivery. If you are willing to spend 5 minutes picking up your order from your local store's parking space, that $99 stays in your pocket.

3. Stacking Gift Cards and Promotions at Target

To extract the greatest value out of Target trips without paying for a subscription, layer weekly promotional gift card promos (such as "Buy 3 household essentials, get a $10 Target Gift Card") with digital manufacturer coupons and your 5% Circle Card discount[span_17](start_span)[span_17](end_span).

1. The 12-Month Rolling Expiration Rule

Standard myWalgreens Cash rewards expire exactly 12 months from the date they were earned[span_14](start_span)[span_14](end_span). For example, if you earned $5.00 in Walgreens Cash on October 15, 2025, that specific $5.00 expires on October 15, 2026.

When you redeem Walgreens Cash at the register, the system automatically uses the oldest points first (First-In, First-Out), preserving your newer reward points.

2. The 6-Month Inactivity Forfeiture Trap

This is where most shoppers lose their rewards. Under official myWalgreens program terms, if you do not scan your myWalgreens account on an active transaction for six consecutive calendar months, your entire accumulated balance is forfeited[span_15](start_span)[span_15](end_span)[span_16](start_span)[span_16](end_span).

  • You do not need to redeem rewards to reset this clock.
  • Buying a simple $0.50 pack of gum or a gallon of milk while scanning your loyalty card counts as an active transaction and protects your points.

3. Paper Register Rewards vs. Digital Walgreens Cash

Do not confuse digital Walgreens Cash with paper Register Rewards printed by the Catalina machine[span_17](start_span)[span_17](end_span)[span_18](start_span)[span_18](end_span):

  • Register Rewards (Manufacturer Slips): Print as paper register vouchers and carry strict expiration dates—often lasting only 7 to 14 days[span_19](start_span)[span_19](end_span)[span_20](start_span)[span_20](end_span). Expired paper slips cannot be reissued or entered into the app.
  • Digital Walgreens Cash: Loads directly into your account and lasts up to a year[span_21](start_span)[span_21](end_span).

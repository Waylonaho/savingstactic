How to Stack Savings & Cashback Without Losing Money

  1. Verify Unit Price First: Never buy a brand-name item just for a $1.00 app rebate if the store brand is still $1.50 cheaper per ounce. Check it in our Unit Price Calculator.
  2. Layer Store Digital Coupons Before Checkout: Clip the store's own digital coupon first so your out-of-pocket register total drops immediately.
  3. Upload the Same Receipt to Non-Conflicting Apps: You can scan one grocery receipt into both Ibotta (for item rebates) and Fetch (for general receipt points) as long as you follow each app's manufacturer coupon rules.
  4. Cash Out Promptly at Minimum Thresholds: Never treat cashback apps like a bank account. Withdraw to PayPal or bank ACH as soon as you hit the $5–$20 threshold.

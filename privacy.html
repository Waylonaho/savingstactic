Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Updated: October 2026

1. Overview

At SavingsTactic.com, we are committed to protecting your online privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect, how it is used, and how we safeguard your browsing experience when interacting with our digital publication and micro-survey tools.

2. Information Collection & Data Separation

SavingsTactic.com does not require user account registration or directly store form-submitted contact details (such as your name, email address, street address, phone number, or payment credentials) on our own database servers. Interactive screening tools and calculators run locally in your browser to help filter relevant programs. However, as detailed in Sections 3 and 4 below, standard web server access logs automatically record technical network identifiers (including IP addresses and browser user-agents), and clicking outbound partner links transmits standard HTTP referral headers and device parameters to those third-party sponsors.

3. Log Files & Analytics Cookies

Like most standard web servers, SavingsTactic.com utilizes standard log files and non-personally identifiable analytical tools (such as Google Analytics or server-level traffic logs). Information gathered includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), referring/exit pages, platform type, date/time stamp, and number of clicks to analyze trends and administer the site.

4. Third-Party Links & Advertiser Disclosures

Our website contains referral links to external third-party offer providers, market research panels, and brand promotions. When you click on an outbound referral link (such as a campaign button), you leave SavingsTactic.com and are redirected to an independent partner page. We have no control over, and assume no responsibility for, the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites or services.

Third-Party Partner & Offer Handoff: SavingsTactic.com features promotional links and interactive screening tools for third-party market research panels (such as ConsumersConnect). When you click an outbound partner link, you leave SavingsTactic.com and navigate to an external domain. We do not collect or store survey response data directly on our servers; however, partner networks receive standard HTTP referral data and device identifiers under their own respective privacy policies.

5. California & Consumer Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

Under applicable consumer privacy acts, residents have the right to request disclosures regarding data usage. Because SavingsTactic.com does not harvest, store, or sell personal data, no personal data profiles exist on our servers to modify or erase.

6. Updates to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect site updates or compliance regulations. Any revisions will be posted directly to this page with an updated modification date.

Dollar General Penny Shopping List 2026: Clearance Dot Schedule & How to Find $0.01 Items

By Savings Tactic Editorial Team • Updated November 27, 2026 • Clearance & Barcode Scanner Guide

Dollar General penny shopping means spotting discontinued seasonal, home, apparel, or reset items that have dropped to $0.01 in the register system after cycling through standard markdown stages. Those penny prices act as a pull-list signal for store staff to clear discontinued merchandise off shelves.

If an unpulled item is still on the shelf and scans at $0.01 at checkout, stores typically honor the $0.01 ring price for items in your cart and may clear remaining stock afterward.

Typical Dollar General Markdown Progression

Stage 1 25%–50% Off Early seasonal drop
Stage 2 70% Off Mid-clearance window
Stage 3 90% Off Late clearance sweep
Stage 4 $0.01 Penny Typically Tuesday flip

1. How to Spot Clearance Categories & Tags in Store

Clearance merchandise often carries colored dots, seasonal icons, or reset stickers that signal which category is aging out. Because color/dot assignments can vary by season and region, use the table below as a practical hunting map rather than a rigid law:

Tag / Visual Clue Common Categories Where to Check
Apparel Dots & Tags Socks, slippers, tees, seasonal wear Clothing racks, bins, and top-shelf overstock
Home & Decor Dots Kitchen gadgets, linens, accent decor, storage Home aisle top shelves and endcaps
Seasonal Icons / Themes Holiday, summer, back-to-school, outdoor/garden Seasonal run, clearance endcaps, and back corners
Planogram (POG) Reset Stickers Discontinued grocery, personal care, or hardware Regular inline aisles tucked behind newer stock

2. How to Scan Items Safely in the Dollar General App

Shelf strips frequently lag behind corporate price updates. Use the in-app barcode scanner as your check:

  1. Select Your Exact Store First: Set your current store location inside the DG app so you see that store's pricing rather than a neighboring store's. See Dollar General App Price Higher at Checkout if prices mismatch.
  2. Scan the Item UPC: Scan the barcode directly on the product packaging—not the shelf label.
  3. Check the Price Readout: If it reads $0.01 (or $0.00 in some app views), verify the item matches the exact SKU before checking out. Stock indicators in apps can lag behind physical shelves.

3. Practical Rules & Store Etiquette for Penny Hunters

  • Don't Ask Staff to "Find" Penny Items: Penny merchandise is meant to be pulled from shelves; asking employees to hunt for penny items often prompts staff to pull remaining stock immediately. Scan quietly and shop respectfully.
  • Check Early on Tuesdays: Weekly markdown updates generally roll out on Tuesday mornings—early visits catch unpulled stock before daytime resets.
  • Keep Aisles Neat & Stay Polite at Checkout: Never rip open sealed packaging or leave clutter behind. If a register question comes up, stay calm and courteous with cashiers and managers.

Dollar General Penny Shopping FAQ

Can I apply manufacturer coupons to $0.01 items?

No. Manufacturer coupons cannot exceed an item's price or generate cash overage, and many digital coupons exclude clearance/penny items.

Why does one store have a penny item that another store doesn't?

Inventory and reset timing vary by store layout, delivery schedule, and regional distribution—one store may already have sold or pulled an item while another still has a stray unit.

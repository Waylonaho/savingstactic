Do Pickup and Delivery Orders Follow the Same Digital Coupon Rules as In-Store?

By Savings Tactic Editorial Team • Updated November 27, 2026 • Covering In-Store, 1st-Party Pickup & 3rd-Party Delivery

No—curbside pickup, same-day delivery, and third-party marketplace orders do not follow identical coupon and reward rules to in-store registers. First-party store apps (like CVS.com, Walgreens.com, and Kroger Pickup) generally honor clipped digital coupons on eligible online carts, whereas paper coupons, out-of-stock substitutions, pickup hold windows, and third-party delivery apps introduce major differences.

2. CVS ExtraBucks & 3rd-Party Delivery Marketplaces

Orders placed directly on CVS.com or in the CVS Pharmacy App with a linked ExtraCare card can apply clipped digital coupons and ExtraBucks on eligible items, whereas orders placed inside third-party delivery apps (such as DoorDash, Instacart, or Uber Eats) run through separate marketplace billing and generally do not redeem CVS ExtraBucks or store-specific threshold coupons.

3. Walgreens Pickup Holds & Reward Expiration Nuances

At Walgreens, uncollected pickup orders are automatically canceled after the hold window (typically 72 hours after ready notification) without charging your payment method. Also keep reward expiration rules in mind: standard Walgreens Cash follows a rolling 12-month expiration window (and a 6-month account inactivity forfeiture rule), while eligible myWalgreens Credit Card rewards earned on or after January 31, 2026 carry a 24-month rolling expiration window.

4. 5 Reasons Clipped Digital Coupons Vanish or Fail

  1. Reserved in an Online Cart: Items sitting in a pending pickup or delivery cart can tie up or show coupons differently than an in-store trip.
  2. Overlapping Rebate Routing: Using one cashback/rewards system on an item can conflict with or reverse overlapping offers depending on offer terms. Check Walmart Cash and Ibotta Together.
  3. Single-Transaction Multi-Use Caps: At retailers like Kroger, promotional multi-use coupons apply up to their stated limit (such as up to 5 times in a single transaction) and then expire after that checkout closes.
  4. Multiple Phone Numbers / Loyalty Profiles: Clipping under one number and scanning another at the register is a frequent cause of missing coupons.

When Do Walgreens Cash Rewards Expire? Dates & Inactivity Rules

By Savings Tactic Editorial Team • Published October 9, 2026

Unlike CVS ExtraBucks, which often expire in 14 to 30 days, Walgreens loyalty currency seems built for long-term saving. But if you have accrued a large balance, you need to know: when do Walgreens Cash rewards expire?

While standard rewards carry a generous 12-month rolling expiration window, thousands of members lose their entire accumulated balance without warning due to Walgreens' strict 6-month account inactivity rule.

1. The 12-Month Rolling Expiration Rule

Standard myWalgreens Cash rewards expire exactly 12 months from the date they were earned. For example, if you earned $5.00 in Walgreens Cash on October 15, 2025, that specific $5.00 expires on October 15, 2026.

When you redeem Walgreens Cash at the register, the system automatically uses the oldest points first (First-In, First-Out), preserving your newer reward points.

2. The 6-Month Inactivity Forfeiture Trap

This is where most shoppers lose their rewards. Under official myWalgreens program terms, if you do not scan your myWalgreens account on an active transaction for six consecutive calendar months, your entire accumulated balance is forfeited.

  • You do not need to redeem rewards to reset this clock.
  • Buying a simple $0.50 pack of gum or a gallon of milk while scanning your loyalty card counts as an active transaction and protects your points.

3. Paper Register Rewards vs. Digital Walgreens Cash

Do not confuse digital Walgreens Cash with paper Register Rewards printed by the Catalina machine:

  • Register Rewards (Manufacturer Slips): Print as paper register vouchers and carry strict expiration dates—often lasting only 7 to 14 days. Expired paper slips cannot be reissued or entered into the app.
  • Digital Walgreens Cash: Loads directly into your account and lasts up to a year.

When spending your rewards on circular promos, follow our rulebook on How to Use Walgreens Cash on Spend Deals to avoid voiding booster rewards at checkout.

Quick Action Checklist Before You Checkout

  1. Check Unit Price First: Compare cost per ounce, pound, or count—never let a coupon or rebate push you into a higher unit price on a bigger or brand-name pack.
  2. Clip & Verify Before Entering the Store: Load digital offers ahead of time, confirm exact size/UPC eligibility in the app, and set your exact store location.
  3. Separate Register Cash From Rebates: Track what you pay out of pocket at checkout separately from pending app rebates or gift card rewards.
  4. Keep Your Receipt Until Rewards Post: Snap or save receipts right away and verify points/rebates before discarding proof of purchase.

2026 Household Unit-Price Quick Reference

Grocery shelf tags frequently mix units—displaying one package per ounce, the neighbor per pound, and paper goods per roll instead of per 100 sheets. Use these conversion rules to spot hidden shrinkflation:

Product Category Best Comparison Unit Common Trap to Avoid
Paper Towels & Bath Tissue Cost per 100 Sheets "Mega Roll" labels vary wildly in sheet count between brands.
Laundry Detergent & Pods Cost per Load (ct) 2X/4X concentrated jugs have fewer fluid ounces but more loads.
Cooking Oils & Beverages Cost per Fluid Ounce (fl oz) 500 mL (16.9 fl oz) vs. 16 fl oz bottles look identical on the shelf.
Pantry Staples, Coffee & Meat Cost per Ounce (oz) Coffee bags shrunk from 16 oz to 12 oz or 10.5 oz while keeping similar bag heights.
